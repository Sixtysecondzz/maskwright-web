PayPal already offers a business side and a personal side for one person or one company. That is the split we will talk about. Competitor blogs write create-multiple-PayPal guides. We will not. Isolation is two browser profiles for two jobs you already have. It is not a second invented wallet.

Local Windows. One entity you are allowed to operate.

## One entity, two jobs

Personal PayPal is friends, marketplace hobby sales, the balance you do not want mixed with SKU payouts. Business PayPal is the store, the invoices, the tax forms. PayPal’s own product already names that split. Your job is not to invent a third.

Put business PayPal in the same MaskWright profile as the store admin that receives those payouts, or in a dedicated money profile for that entity. Put personal PayPal in the daily driver or a personal profile. [Ecommerce accounts on one PC](/blog/ecommerce-accounts-on-one-pc) is the folder map. [Supplier portals versus retail admin](/blog/supplier-portals-vs-retail-admin) is the other mash to avoid: do not pay suppliers from a profile that also holds the store’s PayPal if a contractor uses that supplier window.

## Amazon, affiliates, and what we refuse

[Antidetect for Amazon](/blog/antidetect-browsers-for-amazon) and [Seller Central isolation](/blog/amazon-seller-central-isolation) are marketplace folders. PayPal may still show up for some payouts. Keep it with the entity, not with a research window.

Affiliate desks: [dashboard isolation](/blog/affiliate-dashboard-isolation), [research profiles](/blog/affiliate-research-rooms), [extensions we refuse](/blog/affiliate-extensions-we-refuse), [self-clicks are fraud](/blog/affiliate-self-clicks-are-fraud). A second PayPal to hide self-clicks is not a use I will document. Isolation is so brand A’s cookies do not hit brand B, not so you can mint wallets.

## Disk facts

PayPal cookies live in the Chromium user-data dir. MaskWright encrypts `profiles.json` (AES-256-GCM), not that dir. Exporting cookies exports the wallet session. Do not email it.

| Job | Profile |
| --- | --- |
| Store payouts / business PayPal | Entity / store admin |
| Personal PayPal | Personal |
| Supplier checkout | Supplier, without store PayPal |
| Affiliate research | Cold, no PayPal |

On one Windows PC I have seen people keep personal PayPal in the same Chromium as Shopify admin because “it is all my company.” PayPal’s UI will still offer personal balance next to business if you logged into a mixed identity. The browser folder cannot fix a mixed PayPal login. Log into the business account in the entity profile. Keep friends-and-family in the personal profile. If PayPal still shows both in one login, that is their cluster, like Google. Split what you can split. Do not invent a second email to hide a refund.

Affiliate dashboards that pay to PayPal stay with the affiliate ops profile, not with the cold research profile you use to look at offers. [Self-clicks are fraud](/blog/affiliate-self-clicks-are-fraud) stays refused. Isolation is not a second wallet for that.

Pushback I hear: “PayPal already mixed personal and business in one login.” Then the browser split can only do so much. Follow their upgrade path. Still keep research and supplier checkouts out of that session. Do not invent a second PayPal email to hide a refund. Isolation is two jobs you already have, not a farm of wallets.

## FAQ

### PayPal asked me to upgrade to business. Is that a new profile?

It can stay the same Google-style cluster inside PayPal. Follow their upgrade. You may still keep personal and business browsing in different folders so a research tab does not sit on the business session.

### Can I run two business PayPals for two LLCs I actually own?

If you legally have two entities, two admin profiles can match that. That is not a guide to invent LLCs or wallets. Talk to your accountant.

### Does MaskWright hide PayPal from fingerprinting?

It isolates folders. It does not make a second legal wallet. [Download MaskWright](/download) for the folder split.

### Is PayPal Business a separate cookie store by default?

Only if you log into it in a separate browser profile. PayPal’s own business/personal switch can still sit in one login. The folder split is what we control. Their cluster is theirs.
