If you came here to buy a residential pool, this page is a closed door. MaskWright does not broker traffic, does not meter gigabytes, and does not take a cut of an IPRoyal or Bright Data invoice. Templates in the UI are fill-in strings. They are not a store.

Competitor blogs rank providers because ranking is a storefront. I will point you at evaluation criteria and at the local field where you paste a line you already bought. I will not publish a top ten with coupon codes.

## Why we refuse the checkout

A checkout would make us a second custodian of your bandwidth bill and, often, of your usage graph. It would also tempt the product to steer you toward the pool that pays us. [Proxies in an antidetect browser](/blog/proxies-in-an-antidetect-browser) should stay a launcher feature, not a storefront.

Free antidetect in this category sometimes means “free seats, paid traffic.” Our free is ads in dashboard chrome. Your exit is a separate invoice you chose.

## What the MaskWright field actually accepts

HTTP, HTTPS-as-forwarder, SOCKS5. Formats like `host:port`, `user:pass@host:port`, `type://...`. Geo alignment on launch can hit ip-api.com through the proxy. Credentials sit in encrypted `profiles.json`. We do not upload them.

If a vendor told you that you must buy their residential pack to use their antidetect, that is their business model. It is not a technical requirement of a local profile.
