Use a YouTube Brand Account before you invent another Gmail just to hold a channel. Google already has a model for managers, owners, and a channel that is not your personal watch history. A local profile still helps. It does not replace that model.

The useful caveat: extra Gmail accounts are extra Google gravity. They cluster. Read [YouTube and Google account clusters](/blog/youtube-and-google-account-clusters) before you multiply inboxes.

## Official Brand Account, then a folder

I add managers in YouTube's Brand Account settings. They sign in as themselves. [YouTube Studio on a local profile](/blog/youtube-studio-on-a-local-profile) is where that login lives on Windows. [Multiple accounts on one PC](/blog/multiple-accounts-on-one-pc) is the parent split.

MaskWright 0.1: per-profile `user-data-dir`, Windows 10/11, no seats. `profiles.json` is encrypted. Chromium user data is not. Cookie dumps are not how you add a manager.

## Do not mix other brands' gravity

X brand work is another folder. [X brand accounts on a local profile](/blog/x-brand-accounts-on-a-local-profile). X ads: [X Ads Manager isolation](/blog/x-ads-manager-isolation). Unofficial X helpers: [X API versus unofficial helpers](/blog/x-api-versus-unofficial-helpers). LinkedIn ads and public LinkedIn looking are other folders. [Campaign Manager isolation](/blog/linkedin-ads-manager-isolation). [LinkedIn research on public pages](/blog/linkedin-research-on-public-pages).

Google's account picker will try to keep everything in one browser. That is why the folder exists.

| Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Brand Account + official managers | New Gmail per intern |
| One Studio profile per brand login | Cookie export as onboarding |
| Personal watch history in a personal Google profile | Uploading client videos from personal Gmail |

## How I set it

Empty profile. Sign into the Google account that already owns or manages the Brand Account. Switch to the Brand Account in YouTube. Confirm Studio shows the right channel. Confirm the picker is not a pile of client Gmails.

Optional HTTP or SOCKS5 if that brand already uses an exit. Do not reuse another client's proxy string.

## On a real Windows desk

I create or accept the Brand Account in Google first, on an empty MaskWright profile. I add managers there. I do not create `channel-intern-gmail` as a costume. Interns get manager access on the Brand Account, then their own empty profile on their PC.

Channel art and video files live in ops storage. The profile folder is for cookies, not for the media library. Disk fills up because people dump 4K masters into `user-data-dir`. [Disk use](/blog/disk-use-and-user-data) is a real problem on a small SSD.

Google will offer to add another account when I open Drive. I cancel it. One identity in the picker unless Google already tied Studio and Gmail on purpose.

If X brand posting is the same human, it is still a different folder. Different cookies. Different 2FA story.

## FAQ

### Is a Brand Account enough without MaskWright?

For one channel and one personal life, Chrome profiles can be enough. I use MaskWright when several brands, ads UIs, and research must not share extensions and download trays.

### Can two brands share one Google login?

Google may allow multiple Brand Accounts on one login. If the businesses must not share history, prefer separate Google identities plus separate folders. Follow what you already set up in Google, then isolate the cookie stores.

### Does this hide that I am on Windows?

No. It separates cookie stores. Hardware still exists.

### Strike or termination?

YouTube's process. A new folder is not a restore kit.

### Can I keep YouTube watch history in the Brand Account profile?

Personal watching belongs in personal Google. Brand uploads belong here. Mixing them is how a client channel inherits your hobby recommendations.

Create the Brand Account the way Google documents. Put that Google session in a local folder. Invite managers. Do not invent extra Gmails as a costume.

I skip extra intern Gmails, cookie onboarding, and dumping 4K masters into `user-data-dir`. I watch the account picker. I add managers in Google, not by mailing a folder. Desktop Client Hints stay desktop.

I confirm the Brand Account in YouTube before every upload, even when I think I am already on it. Google switchers lie by being quiet. One extra Gmail in the picker is enough to put a client video on the wrong channel.
