I read public LinkedIn pages in a profile that has never held Recruiter, Navigator, or Campaign Manager cookies. If the page is public in a logged-out browser, I can open it. If it is not, I stop. I will not write an inbox scrape.

That is the useful caveat. Competitor posts in this query are often scraper guides. This one is headed research on what LinkedIn already shows the public. Authorized work only.

## What counts as public here

Company Pages that load without a login. Posts LinkedIn marks public. Directory pages that do not demand a seat. Notes I type into a document I own.

Not: Recruiter search dumps. Not: InMail. Not: a script that pages through result lists. [Recruiter workflows](/blog/linkedin-recruiter-workflows) stay licensed. [Sales Navigator stays licensed](/blog/linkedin-sales-navigator-stays-licensed).

[Multiple accounts on one PC](/blog/multiple-accounts-on-one-pc) is the parent split. MaskWright 0.1: Windows 10/11, empty `user-data-dir` for this job. No Playwright product. Cookie import files are plaintext. I import nothing into research.

## How I set the research profile

Empty cookies. Usually logged out. Chromium core I will keep. Locale matches the market I am reading, or the household. No Insight Tags. No ATS extensions. Optional HTTP or SOCKS5 only if the desk already uses one for public looking. MaskWright does not bundle exits.

If LinkedIn shows a login wall, I do not invent a throwaway identity to walk through it. I use a licensed seat I already pay for, in that seat's folder, or I stop.

| Research profile | Never |
| --- | --- |
| Public HTML, notes, screenshots I took | Recruiter / Navigator cookies |
| Logged out when the page allows | Campaign Manager |
| Optional looking-proxy | Inbox exporters |

## What I will not document

Selectors. Headless paging. How to hide a collector. If LinkedIn forbade automated collection, a headed window is not a costume for the same job.

## On a real Windows desk

The research profile starts empty every time I mean it to stay cold. I do not import cookies "from a throwaway." I do not sign into LinkedIn unless a public page actually demanded it, and then I still do not use Recruiter.

Notes go in Obsidian or a doc, not in a form I submit to a shady exporter. Screenshots go to ops. The Chromium download tray gets emptied. If I hit a login wall, I stop or I open the licensed seat I already pay for, in that seat's folder.

I skip headless paging. I skip selector packs. If I need structured public data, I check whether LinkedIn's official APIs cover it. A headed window is for reading, not for dressing a loop.

Optional looking-proxy: geo check, then browse. I do not attach the company's Navigator sticky exit to research because it was still in the clipboard.

## FAQ

### Can I use my personal LinkedIn to look?

You can. I often do not, because that history then lives on the identity I also use for people I know. An empty logged-out profile is cheaper to throw away.

### Is this the same as Google-indexing LinkedIn?

If Google already shows a snippet, you can read Google. If you open LinkedIn, you are on LinkedIn's terms. I do not treat a SERP as a scrape license.

### Do I need Sales Navigator for public Pages?

No. Public Pages are public. Navigator is a SKU for deeper search you pay for.

### Cloud browser for research?

Then the vendor sees the looking. I keep even research on local disk when I can. [Compare local versus cloud](/compare).

### Can I save public Pages to a Recruiter project from the research profile?

That is using Recruiter. Open the licensed seat for licensed work. Research stays logged out when the page allows it.
