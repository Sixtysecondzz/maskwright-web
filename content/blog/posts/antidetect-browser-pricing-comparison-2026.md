## GoLogin Pricing

**Free Tier:** 3 profiles
**Paid Plans:** $30-100+/month (100-1000+ profiles)
**Architecture:** Cloud-based

GoLogin offers a generous free tier for testing. You get 3 profiles with full features. This is enough for small-scale operations or testing the platform.

Paid plans scale with profile count:
- 100 profiles: ~$30/month
- 500 profiles: ~$60/month
- 1000+ profiles: ~$100+/month

**Pros:**
- 3 free profiles (good for testing)
- Cloud sync (access from any device)
- Team features on paid plans
- Mobile fingerprints available

**Cons:**
- Subscription required for serious use
- Cloud storage (vendor breach risk)
- No local-only option

**Best for:** Users who need cloud access and team features, willing to pay for subscription.

## Hidden Costs to Consider

**Subscription Creep**
- Most tools increase prices as you add profiles
- Free tiers are limited (2-10 profiles)
- Serious use requires paid plans

**Cloud Dependency**
- If vendor goes down, you lose access
- If vendor is breached, your profiles are exposed
- If vendor changes pricing, you must pay or migrate

**Local Storage**
- You are responsible for backups
- You are responsible for security (disk encryption)
- No cloud access (profiles live on one PC)
