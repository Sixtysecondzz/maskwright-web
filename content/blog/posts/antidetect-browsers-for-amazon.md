If you searched "best antidetect browser for Amazon," you probably wanted extra seller accounts. That is not what I will sell. An antidetect browser on this desk is a local Windows launcher that keeps Seller Central, supplier portals, and public looking in different cookie stores. Ops live on [Amazon Seller Central isolation](/blog/amazon-seller-central-isolation).

The useful caveat: MaskWright 0.1 does not make two selling accounts look unrelated. Amazon's related-account rules are about identity and activity. Isolation is hygiene for work you are already allowed to do.

## Why local versus cloud for Amazon

Seller Central is the store. A cloud browser holds a second copy of that session. MaskWright keeps `user-data-dir` on your Windows 10/11 disk. `profiles.json` is encrypted. The Chromium tree is not. No account, no seats, no bundled residential pack. Bring your own HTTP or SOCKS5 if that entity already uses an exit. Sticky with admin. Do not rotate under a live Seller Central login.

Custom Chromium 131 when the core is installed, otherwise system Chrome or Edge fallback. Desktop profiles only. No Android emulation.

I do not publish trophy scores. Amazon can still see payments, documents, and behavior. Isolate cookies because jobs should not mix.

Often. Use MaskWright when supplier, research, and a second authorized storefront must not share extensions.

No. Desktop admin is desktop. This product models desktop Client Hints.

No.

No. A cloud phone is someone else's device with your login. This product has no Android runtime. Seller Central on desktop is a desktop job.
