Competitor pages treat a Cloudflare wall as a puzzle. Ours treat it as a stop, then a search for the official channel. I will not help you around the wall. This is the ethics page for that class of sign.

I will not name bypass products. I will not list challenge types as a menu. [What web scraping is](/blog/what-is-web-scraping) already said a wall ends the HTML job.

## What the wall is saying

The publisher put a gate in front of the HTML. Sometimes it is clumsy. Sometimes it sits on a marketing page a person can pass. Sometimes it sits on an API you were never invited to hit from a browser. In every case the gate is a published preference: this path is not for your client right now.

[Captchas are a stop sign](/blog/captchas-are-a-stop-sign) is the puzzle-grid sibling. An interstitial, a JavaScript challenge, a managed wall: same class. [Rate limits are not a puzzle](/blog/rate-limits-are-not-a-puzzle) is the numeric sibling. Do not rotate exits until the wall goes away.

[robots.txt and terms come first](/blog/robots-txt-and-terms-come-first). A wall does not rewrite a Disallow. It adds a stop on top.

## Official channels after the stop

Look for the door the publisher did want. A documented API. A partner program. A public export. A Library UI a person can use. Official channels beat headed collection for the same bytes. Public Ads Library research stays on that UI. [Facebook Ads Library research locally](/blog/facebook-ads-library-research-locally). A Python client starts from those libraries, not from a challenge solver. [Web scraping with Python, without a stealth stack](/blog/web-scraping-with-python-overview).

If you own the host, you own the allowlist. [Playwright for pages you own](/blog/playwright-for-pages-you-own). Fix staging. Do not practice bypass on a stranger's wall and call it rehearsal.

If the bytes sit behind a login, you were already in the wrong job. [Scraping behind a login is not research](/blog/scraping-behind-login-is-not-research). Personal data you have no right to hold stays refused. [Personal data and collection](/blog/personal-data-and-collection).

| Wall on... | What I do |
| --- | --- |
| A host I operate | Fix the allowlist, whitelist CI, ticket it |
| A public marketing page, me as a person | Try later as a person, or read another way |
| A collector I wrote | Stop. Look for the official API |
| A login I was not invited to automate | Stop. Not research |

## Your wall versus their wall

On a host you operate, a challenge you bought is a configuration problem. You add the CI IP, you turn the rule down on staging, you fix the bot fight you started with yourself. The leftover is a ticket, not a bypass note.

On a host you do not operate, the same challenge is a published stop. You do not have the Cloudflare dashboard. You have the official API docs, a public UI, or nothing. Treating their wall as a lab is how hide playbooks get written. I will not write them.

A person in a cold profile who hits a wall on a marketing page can try again later as a person, or read the same copy another way. A program that hits the wall is done.

## Local desk

When a cold profile hits a wall, close it. Write "wall at URL, time" in the research folder. Do not open the work profile to click through so a script can resume. That is a person-as-solver pattern.

MaskWright 0.1 has no anti-bot integration and no solver hook. A coherent fingerprint on a work profile is for authorized logins, not for clearance at a harvest wall. I will not document using the profile that way.

The ethics list is the parent. This URL owns one line: a wall is a stop, then the official door, then nothing.

If a vendor demo clears the wall in the thumbnail, close the tab. The demo is selling the puzzle. You already have the answer.

The [Scraping](/blog/scraping) hub is built to disappoint the bypass query. If you arrived from a Cloudflare how-to, the useful next click is the API docs, not a disguise.

Keep a one-line log when a cold profile hits a wall: host, time, signed-out. That log is enough to decide whether an official channel exists. It is not a clearance cookbook. Do not store cookies from the challenge page. Do not screenshot the puzzle as if it were a trophy.
