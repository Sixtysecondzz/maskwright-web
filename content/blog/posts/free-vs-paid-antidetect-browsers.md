## What free tiers actually include

Free tiers are not all the same. Here is what typically gets restricted:

### Profile count

This is the obvious one. Free tiers cap how many profiles you can create.

- MaskWright: Unlimited
- Incogniton: 10
- Dolphin Anty: 10
- GoLogin: 3
- AdsPower: 2
- Multilogin: 0

If you run 2 to 5 profiles for your own work, most free tiers suffice. If you run 20 profiles for an ecommerce operation, you need paid plans (or MaskWright).

### Team features

Free tiers almost never include team collaboration.

- MaskWright: No team features (by design, even on free tier)
- Incogniton: Team features require paid plans
- Dolphin Anty: Team seats cost extra ($10-20/seat)
- GoLogin: Team sharing requires paid plans
- AdsPower: Team collaboration requires paid plans
- Multilogin: Team features require paid plans

If you need to share profiles with team members, you are paying regardless of which tool you choose (except MaskWright, which does not offer team features at all).

### Cloud access

Cloud-based tools often restrict cloud launches on free tiers.

- MaskWright: No cloud (local-only by design)
- Incogniton: Local browser free, cloud sync requires paid
- Dolphin Anty: Cloud access included but limited by profile count
- GoLogin: Cloud browser available on free tier (3 profiles)
- AdsPower: Cloud access included but limited by profile count
- Multilogin: Cloud phones require paid plans

If you need to launch profiles from multiple devices, you need a cloud tool and likely a paid plan.

### Built-in proxies

Most free tiers do not include meaningful proxy allowances.

- MaskWright: No built-in proxies (bring your own, even on free tier)
- Incogniton: One-time proxy bonus, no recurring allowance
- Dolphin Anty: 1.5 GB free proxy bonus (one-time), no recurring
- GoLogin: No free proxies, must purchase separately
- AdsPower: No free proxies, must purchase separately
- Multilogin: No free proxies, must purchase separately

If you need residential or mobile proxies, you are paying extra regardless of tool. MaskWright requires you to bring your own from the start.

### API access

Automation APIs are almost always restricted to paid plans.

- MaskWright: No API (by design)
- Incogniton: API requires paid plans
- Dolphin Anty: API requires paid plans
- GoLogin: REST API requires paid plans (300+ RPM at higher tiers)
- AdsPower: API requires paid plans
- Multilogin: API requires paid plans

If you run automated workflows with Selenium or Puppeteer, you need a paid plan (or a different tool).

### Support priority

Free tier users often get lower-priority support.

- MaskWright: Community support, documentation
- Incogniton: Email support, slower response for free users
- Dolphin Anty: Live chat available, but free users report slower response

## When free is enough

Free tiers are sufficient if:

- **You run under 10 profiles.** Incogniton and Dolphin Anty give you 10 free profiles. MaskWright gives you unlimited.
- **You are a solo operator.** You do not need team features, permissions, or cloud sync.
- **You run Windows only.** MaskWright is Windows-only and free. If that matches your setup, you are covered.
- **You already have proxies.** You do not need built-in proxy packages. You can source your own residential or ISP proxies.
- **You do not need automation APIs.** You are a human operator clicking through profiles, not running scripted workflows.
- **You are testing or learning.** You want to evaluate antidetect browsers before committing to a paid plan.

If you fit these criteria, free tiers are genuinely usable. MaskWright covers unlimited profiles for Windows users. Incogniton and Dolphin Anty cover up to 10 profiles for cross-platform users.

## When you need to pay

Paid plans become necessary if:

- **You run 20+ profiles.** Free tiers cap out at 10 profiles (or 3 for GoLogin, 2 for AdsPower).
- **You need team collaboration.** You need to share profiles with team members, set permissions, track activity.
- **You need cloud access.** You launch profiles from multiple devices, or you need cloud phones for mobile fingerprints.
- **You need automation APIs.** You run Selenium, Puppeteer, or custom scripts against the browser.
- **You want built-in proxies.** You do not want to source your own proxies. You prefer bundled residential or mobile IPs.
- **You need priority support.** You run a business where downtime costs money. You want fast response times.

If any of these apply, you are looking at paid plans. The question becomes: which tool offers the best value?

## The hidden costs of free

Free tiers have hidden costs beyond feature restrictions:

### Vendor lock-in

Cloud-based free tiers lock your profiles to their infrastructure. If they change pricing, shut down, or have an outage, you are affected.

MaskWright stores profiles locally. You control the data. No vendor lock-in.

### Migration costs

If you start with a free cloud tier and outgrow it, migrating to a different tool requires exporting cookies, recreating profiles, and testing. This takes time and risks session loss.

MaskWright profiles live on your disk from day one. No migration needed if you stay on Windows.

### Opportunity cost

Free tiers with low profile caps (2-3 profiles) force you to upgrade faster. You might pay for a tool you do not fully use because the free tier was too restrictive.

MaskWright and Incogniton/Dolphin Anty (10 profiles) let you scale organically without forced upgrades.

### Security tradeoffs

Some free tiers cut corners on security. Dolphin Anty experienced a major data leak in July 2022 where 15% of user profiles were stolen. They compensated affected users, but the risk was real.

MaskWright stores profiles locally on your disk. Encrypted at rest. Your security depends on your own machine: disk encryption, physical access control, backup discipline.
