## How to choose

Ask yourself these questions:

### Do you need team features?

If yes, you need a cloud tool: GoLogin, AdsPower, Incogniton, Dolphin Anty, or Multilogin. MaskWright does not offer team features.

### Do you need cloud access?

If you need to launch profiles from multiple devices, you need a cloud tool. MaskWright is local-only.

### Do you need mobile fingerprints?

If you run mobile-heavy workflows (TikTok, Instagram mobile, mobile ads), you need cloud phones. Multilogin is the leader here. GoLogin and AdsPower also offer mobile emulation.

### Do you want unlimited free profiles?

MaskWright is the only tool offering unlimited free profiles. Incogniton and Dolphin Anty offer 10 free profiles each.

### Are you Windows-only?

If you run Windows and stay on one machine, MaskWright is a strong fit. Local storage, no subscription, no account required.

### Do you need automation APIs?

If you run Selenium, Puppeteer, or custom scripts, you need a paid plan from a cloud tool. MaskWright does not offer an API.

## FAQ

### What is the best free antidetect browser for Windows?

MaskWright is the best free option for Windows. Unlimited profiles. Local storage. No subscription. Incogniton and Dolphin Anty offer 10 free profiles each but are cloud-based.

### Can I use antidetect browsers on Windows 10 and 11?

Yes. All major antidetect browsers support Windows 10 and 11. MaskWright is Windows-only. GoLogin, AdsPower, Incogniton, and Dolphin Anty also support macOS and Linux.

### Do I need a subscription for antidetect browsers?

No. MaskWright is free with unlimited profiles. Incogniton and Dolphin Anty offer 10 free profiles each. GoLogin offers 3 free profiles. Paid subscriptions are only needed if you outgrow free tiers or need advanced features.

### What is the difference between local and cloud antidetect browsers?

Local browsers (MaskWright) store profiles on your disk. You control the data. Cloud browsers (GoLogin, Multilogin) store profiles on vendor infrastructure. You can access them from any device, but you do not control the hardware.

### Can I migrate profiles between antidetect browsers?

Yes. Export cookies from your current browser as JSON. Create matching profiles in the new browser. Import cookies. Test thoroughly before decommissioning old profiles.

### Do antidetect browsers work with Facebook, Google, TikTok?

Yes, if used correctly. Antidetect browsers isolate fingerprints and cookies. However, you still need to follow platform terms, use authorized accounts, and maintain proper proxy/fingerprint hygiene.

### What is the cheapest antidetect browser for 100 profiles?

GoLogin Professional plan at $49/month (100 profiles) is the cheapest paid option. MaskWright is $0/month (unlimited profiles) but does not offer cloud features.

### Is MaskWright safe for business use?

Yes. MaskWright encrypts profiles at rest on your local disk. Your security depends on your own machine: disk encryption, physical access control, and backup discipline. If you need ISO-certified security, consider AdsPower or enterprise cloud tools.
