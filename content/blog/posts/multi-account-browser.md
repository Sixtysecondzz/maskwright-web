Vendors stretched "multi-account" until it meant a factory. On this site it means a browser that can keep authorized logins in separate profiles on one PC. The accounts already exist. You already have a right to open them. The software's job is to stop those profiles from sharing cookies and a fingerprint record.

If you searched for a create-unlimited guide, this is the wrong page and the wrong site. The category still needs a clean definition. This is that definition, filed in [Antidetect browsers](/blog/antidetect-browsers).

## The job, said without a volume playbook

A founder with two legal entities needs two storefront admins that never share a pixel. An agency contractor needs a client Ads Manager that never inherits the contractor's personal Facebook. A researcher needs a cold profile that never sees customer CSV exports. Those are multiple accounts. They are also ordinary.

[What an antidetect browser is](/blog/what-is-an-antidetect-browser) is the category noun. Multi-account is the search phrase people type when they mean that noun. I keep them in the same hub because the internet already mashed them.

What I will not write: how to mint extra identities, how to age a graph, how to keep a disabled login alive. Platform roles exist so a second person can work inside an account the business already owns. Extra logins that exist only to dodge a limit are a terms problem.

## Multi-account is not multi-session

[A multi-session browser](/blog/multi-session-browser) stacks logins inside one Chrome window. Support desks like that. QA people like that. It shares an engine family. It is a different product class.

[Firefox containers versus antidetect](/blog/firefox-containers-vs-antidetect) borrowed the multi-account name for colored cookie labels inside one Firefox. [Chrome profiles versus an antidetect profile](/blog/chrome-profiles-vs-antidetect) is a lasting Chrome user. Both are weaker than a dedicated user-data directory with a fingerprint record.

| Product class | What you get | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-session Chrome | Several logins in one window | Support, QA, staff tabs |
| Chrome / Firefox users | Some cookie split, shared engine | Personal vs work Google |
| Containers | Cookie colors in one Firefox | Personal hygiene |
| Multi-account antidetect | Isolated directories and seeds | Authorized businesses on one PC |

## What has to stay split

Cookies, localStorage, IndexedDB. If two authorized accounts share those, the site already has the keys.

The fingerprint record. Canvas, WebGL, fonts, screen, timezone, language. A new random paint every morning is a lab habit. Work profiles need a stable seed.

The exit, if you attach one. HTTP or SOCKS5 on the profile, not a system VPN wrapping every login plus personal Chrome.

Extensions. Store A's shipping add-on does not belong in Store B.

[The Chromium core in an antidetect browser](/blog/chromium-core-in-an-antidetect-browser) and [Firefox versus Chromium cores](/blog/firefox-vs-chromium-antidetect-cores) cover the engines. The public channel is Windows 10 and 11 x64. [Antidetect browsers for Windows](/blog/antidetect-browser-for-windows) holds that limit.

## Authorized only, and what that looks like on a desk

Brand Instagram the company owns. Personal Facebook that must never see the brand pixel. A Business Manager you were invited into. A supplier portal for an entity you registered. A community Discord you moderate. Those belong here.

A second seller ID you opened because the first one hit a policy limit does not belong here. Neither does a stack of aged social graphs. [Antidetect versus Tor](/blog/antidetect-browser-vs-tor) is routing, not a way to mint identities.

MaskWright has no team seats. Share work with platform roles. Do not mail a user-data folder. There is no account and no profile sync. `profiles.json` is encrypted. Chromium user-data directories are not.

## When you already have a multi-account browser and it is called Chrome

One personal life and one work Google. Stay in Chrome. The avatar menu is enough. This category starts to earn disk when the desk already holds several authorized identities that must not share cookies.

If you only needed side-by-side staff logins in one window, you wanted multi-session, not this page.

## FAQ

### Is a multi-account browser for creating extra accounts?

Not on this site. It is for keeping authorized logins from sharing cookies and a fingerprint record on one PC.

### How is this different from Chrome profiles?

Chrome users share a binary and a hardware story. An antidetect profile is a dedicated directory plus a fingerprint record and an optional per-profile proxy.

### Can I run several authorized logins at once?

MaskWright can bulk start and stop selected profiles. That is operator convenience. It is not a volume playbook. RAM and disk are the real caps.
