I look at public Instagram pages in a profile that has never held a brand login. That is the whole method. No scraper. No export of someone else's followers. No mixing the research tab with Business Suite cookies.

One thing worth flagging: a headed browser is still a browser. If Instagram shows the page to a logged-out visitor, I can open it. If the page requires a login I do not have, I stop. This is not a collection guide.

## Why a separate profile

Brand cookies are expensive. They hold inbox, ads, and publisher state. Research history is cheap and noisy. I do not want competitor grids, saved posts, and random explore junk sitting next to the brand session.

[Multiple accounts on one PC](/blog/multiple-accounts-on-one-pc) is the parent split. [Instagram cookies and session hygiene](/blog/instagram-cookies-and-session-hygiene) is for sessions you already created. This page is the empty profile those two assume for looking.

MaskWright 0.1: one `user-data-dir` per profile, Windows 10/11, no cloud sync. `profiles.json` is encrypted. The Chromium directory is not. Cookie import files are plaintext. I do not import anything into research.

## What counts as research here

Public profiles. Public posts. Public ads you can already see in official libraries. Notes I type into a document I own.

Not: unofficial exporters, follower dumps, inbox scrapes, or a script that pages through a grid faster than a person. [Instagram scheduling without unofficial helpers](/blog/instagram-scheduling-without-unofficial-helpers) is the sibling refusal for tools that want the session. Research tools that want the session get the same no.

If I need to log in to see something, I use an account I am allowed to use, and I still keep that login out of the brand Suite profile. Usually I stay logged out.

## How I set the research profile

Empty cookies. Chromium core I will keep using. Locale that matches the market I am looking at, or the household if I am not attaching a proxy. No brand extensions. No shipping addons. No pixel helpers.

I do not paste a mobile user agent onto this desktop profile. [TikTok web versus the app](/blog/tiktok-web-versus-the-app) is the same warning on another product: web is not the phone app. Instagram web is not the Instagram app. [Instagram and shared device signals](/blog/instagram-and-shared-device-signals) still exist on one Windows PC.

If the desk already uses a proxy for public looking, I attach HTTP or SOCKS5 I already pay for. MaskWright does not bundle exits. I do not attach the brand's ads exit to research "because it is already in the form."

| Research profile holds | Research profile never holds |
| --- | --- |
| Public pages, notes, screenshots I took on purpose | Brand Instagram / Suite cookies |
| Locale that matches the look | Customer CSVs, payout tabs |
| Optional proxy the desk already uses for looking | Unofficial downloader extensions |
| Empty cookie store at the start | Ads Manager for a brand I run |

## Keep Suite and ads elsewhere

[Meta Business Suite isolation](/blog/instagram-meta-business-suite-isolation) is the control plane. [TikTok Ads Manager isolation](/blog/tiktok-ads-manager-isolation) is the same idea on TikTok. Research does not open those UIs.

If I screenshot a public ad, the screenshot lives in an ops folder, not in the brand profile's download tray. Chrome saves into the profile directory unless I change it. That is how a "quick look" becomes a mixed folder.

## What I will not document

I will not paste selectors. I will not paste a Python loop. I will not document how to hide a collector. If the site forbade automated collection, a headed window is not a costume for the same job.

TikTok device checks are a different surface. [TikTok device checks we can see](/blog/tiktok-device-checks-we-can-see) is that note. I do not treat a desktop profile as a phone.

## FAQ

### Can I use the brand account to look at competitors?

You can. I do not. The brand account then carries that history, those follows, and those ads you clicked. A logged-out research profile is cheaper to throw away.

### Do I need to stay logged out?

For public pages, yes when the page allows it. If a checkpoint appears, I do not invent a throwaway login to bypass it. I stop or I use an account I am allowed to use, still not the brand Suite profile.

### Is this the same as Incognito?

Incognito is one process with a temporary store. Close it and the cookies may go. The everyday profile still sits underneath. A dedicated MaskWright profile is a folder you can keep empty on purpose, with its own proxy field if you use one.

### Can I clone the brand profile and delete cookies?

Deleting cookies from a copy is easy to get wrong. I create a new empty profile instead. Less chance a leftover IndexedDB row survives.
