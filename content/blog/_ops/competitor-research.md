# Competitor research (public blogs only)

Sources reviewed in August 2026:

- https://gologin.com/blog/
- https://multilogin.com/blog/

We extracted topics, categories, and search intent. We did not copy article bodies.

## What they publish

GoLogin's recent home is heavy on cloud phone reviews (LDCloud, UgPhone, BitCloudPhone, VSPhone, VMOS, Redfinger, MoreLogin) plus product updates. Evergreen inventory is "what is an antidetect browser," SMM roundups, TikTok multi-account and unblock guides, scraping browser lists, and fingerprint test recaps.

Multilogin clusters are labeled on the blog itself: Ads, Affiliate marketing, Browser fingerprints, Cloud phone, Comparisons and reviews, Cookie management, Crypto, Dropshipping and e-commerce, Guides, Multiple accounting, Proxies and scraping, Social media marketing, Web automation, Web scraping.

Recent Multilogin intent is even more commercial: Facebook Ads Library scraping, TikTok algorithm, LinkedIn prospecting and automation tools, Reddit IP banned, DuoPlus cloud phone review.

Publishing cadence on both homes is several posts a week, often 8 to 14 minute reads, with category chips and a load-more index. Internal links push toward cloud phones, bundled proxies, and team or API products.

## Search intent they own

- How to run multiple social accounts without linking
- How to fix or avoid platform bans and IP blocks
- Best antidetect / scraping / residential proxy lists
- Cloud phone versus physical phone farm
- Playwright / Puppeteer / Selenium on a stealth browser
- Affiliate, dropshipping, and ads ops at volume

## Gaps we can win without copying them

1. Local vault versus cloud locker. They underwrite this because their product is the locker.
2. Windows-real surfaces: ClearType canvas, client rects, host fonts, WebGPU, IPv6 on Windows DNS.
3. BYO proxy ethic. They upsell exits. We refuse the cut.
4. Official APIs, platform roles, and refused evasion. They rank on evasion intent with thin disclaimers.
5. No team seats, no cloud phones, no bandwidth kit. Their July and August calendars are phone reviews. Ours can be the honest alternative.
6. Operator hygiene: clean stops, download trays, encryption at rest, what actually leaves the machine.

## How we outperform

Same audience (operators who isolate work on a browser). Better coverage means deeper mechanism pages, clearer refusals, and a complete cluster for every platform they treat as a farm tutorial. Each platform note answers the ranking query, then spends the length on authorized rooms, jars, exits, and the work we will not do.

## Formats we use that they underuse

- Architecture tables (local vs cloud, API vs headed, sticky vs rotate)
- FAQ schema on every note
- Category hubs with a point of view, not a tag dump
- First-person bench voice with named desks, not a vendor we voice
