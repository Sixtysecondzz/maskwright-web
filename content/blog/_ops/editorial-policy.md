# MaskWright blog editorial policy

This bench writes for operators who already have a right to the accounts they open. The blog has to be stronger than GoLogin and Multilogin on technical depth, honesty, and local-first architecture. It also has to stay fit for a publisher that wants Google AdSense.

## Product facts

- Windows 10 and 11 first.
- Local encrypted store. No MaskWright account. No cloud sync.
- No team seats. No cloud phones. No bundled proxy.
- Bring your own HTTP or SOCKS5.
- Purpose-built Chromium core on the operator PC.
- Free app. Optional ads live in dashboard chrome we draw, not inside launched tabs.
- We dropped bandwidth-sharing SDKs.

## Voice

- First person I and we.
- Short sentences. Concrete desks, folders, exits, jars.
- No em dashes. No en dashes. Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
- Do not copy or rewrite competitor articles.
- Do not invent search volumes, test scores, or legal advice.

## Allowed angles

- How fingerprints, cookies, and proxies work on a real Windows box.
- Isolation for storefronts, ads accounts, and brand pages the operator is authorized to run.
- Why a VPN is not a fingerprint tool.
- Why cloud browsers and cloud phones hold a second copy of the session.
- Official APIs, platform roles, and slow public-page research.
- Hygiene: leaks, timezone match, sticky exits, clean stops, backups.

## Refused angles

- How to evade a ban, shadowban, checkpoint, or spend limit.
- How to create fake people, aged logins, or engagement farms.
- How to hide a scraper, solver, or driver from a site that forbade the work.
- How to pass CreepJS, Pixelscan, or IPhey as a trophy.
- Warmup scripts, mouse curves, or captcha farms.
- Buying or recycling Business Managers, seller IDs, or social graphs.

When a competitor title is "how to run 50 TikTok accounts safely," we write "how a brand keeps authorized TikTok rooms from sharing a jar, and why we will not teach a farm."

## Article shape

- 1,200 words minimum. 1,500 to 2,500 preferred.
- Title in frontmatter only. Body starts with a paragraph, not an H1.
- Clear H2 headings. H3 inside FAQ.
- One table when it clarifies a comparison.
- FAQ with 3 to 5 questions.
- 2 to 4 images using `![alt](/blog/file.png "Caption")`.
- 3 to 8 markdown links to other `/blog/{slug}` notes.
- At least one link to `/blog/category/{category}`.
- At least one link to `/download` or `/compare`.
